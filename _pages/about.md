# Personal Details

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io), [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://www.ust.hk) ([HKUST](https://www.ust.hk)), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://junxian-h.github.io). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn) ([SJTU](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn)) in June 2024. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.
